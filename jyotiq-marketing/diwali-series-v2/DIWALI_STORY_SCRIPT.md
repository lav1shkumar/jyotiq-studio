# पाँच दीये, पाँच "कब" · Diwali story series (script draft, 6 Nov 2026)

Replaces the scripts in `diwali-d1` to `diwali-d5`. This is script only: nothing has been voiced, generated or rendered.

**The idea:** the same five days, but each reel is now one full story that turns on the question **"कब?"** A bride gets told the night. A demon asks who can kill him, but never when. Bharat counts down fourteen years. A village waits for the rain to stop. A sister keeps asking her brother when he'll visit.

"कब" is also what Jyotiq answers (Amit's bio change from "शादी किससे होगी" to "शादी कब होगी"), so the app ending grows out of the story instead of being added after it.

## Before you read: three things to decide

1. **Length.** These have 198-228 spoken words. At the reference's pace (roughly 2.8 words a second) that's about 75-85 s including the end hold, and closer to 90-100 s if the voice reads slower. Either way it's over the house 54-58 s. The reference runs 116 s, and a story with a real turn doesn't fit in 57 s. Each day has a "first cuts" list that saves about 15 s. **Getting under 60 s would need about 150 words at that pace, which means a rewrite rather than trims.** One viral reel doesn't prove the longer length will work for Jyotiq, so test it on Day 1 and compare watch time. These timings are estimates until voiced.
2. **Dates.** Amit's table may be a day off for the last two reels. See "Posting dates" at the end. Dhanteras was today, and the evening muhurat has already passed.
3. **Narrator.** I used Narad, the founder's own option B for story reels. That needs a new voice and new character art. Using Kaal costs nothing new: drop "नारायण नारायण" and the "मैं वहीं था" asides, and every other word still works.

## How to read the tables

- The line column uses ElevenLabs v3 tags in brackets. Keep the words as written once approved.
- Each day ends with the voiced download line, then the end card: logo, www.jyotiq.ai, Play / App Store badges, "App ka link profile (bio) mein" with the arrow, "Pehla sawaal free · phir ₹5 per sawaal", and the disclaimer. Tomorrow's tease is on-screen text only, so the last thing heard is always the download.
- Hook text is complete from frame 0, in the middle third, on a lit frame. A short title chip (पाँच दीये · दिन N) sits on screen during the hook, so no spoken series intro slows the story.

## What was taken from the reference, in concept only

The reference (`~/Downloads/kilavde 3.mp4`, 115.8 s) is **another creator's Diwali series, and this reel is its Day 3**: Alakshmi, Lakshmi's elder sister. Viewers may see both in the same week, so the line between us and it matters more than usual.

**Why it works**

| What it does | Detail |
|---|---|
| Hook | One surprising fact about a figure everyone knows, then it moves the stakes to your door ("who gets in is decided by your house"). All in 13 s, before any series title. |
| Real story | A character with a problem and a turn (a sage marries her, then finds out what that means) with some humour, not a list of facts. |
| Payoff in your own house | The story explains things viewers already do: cleaning corners, lighting lamps, nimbu-mirchi. |
| One line to repeat | It closes on a short quotable reframe of Amavasya. |
| Narration | Close, direct, short sentences, roughly 2.8 words a second (estimated from Whisper's timed transcript). Our Day 1 ran about 1.9 with pauses, so the pace is a big part of the upgrade. |
| Picture | Rich layered art in constant camera motion. Only about 18 cuts detected across 116 s, so it doesn't rely on a cut every 2 s. Text arrives in short two-colour phrases. |
| Sound (measured with earshot, not heard by a person) | -14.1 LUFS, loudness range only 3.3 LU, so the voice stays very forward. Short drops in the bed around 0:09.95 and 1:19 set up lines. Thunderstorm ambience under the Day 4 tease. Earshot's music labels were low-confidence, so I'm not describing the genre. |
| Weak spot we fix | It ends on "follow for Day 4" with no product. Our ending has to convert, so the story is built to lead there. |

**What we keep (the concept and the quality bar):** a surprising hook about something familiar, a real story with a turn, a payoff in the viewer's own habits, one quotable line per reel, faster narration, and richer art with constant motion.

## Facts and risks to check

Everything below is tradition. Lines are framed with "कहते हैं" where they aren't from a main text, but viewers will still correct us in the comments.

| Day | Item | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hima's son, the gold and lamps, Yama as a serpent listening to songs | Common Dhanteras folk story with no single text. Versions differ on whether light or song stopped him; we use both |
| 1, 2, 3, 5 | "कब?", "तो मेरे पास तीन दिन हैं", "मैं वहीं था", Yamuna's exact words and similar | **Dramatised dialogue and Narad's witness framing are ours**, not scripture |
| 2 | Boon that only his mother can kill him; Satyabhama as Bhudevi; Krishna falling | Well attested in popular tellings, not the Bhagavata's plainer account. Some versions say Krishna pretended; the line only says he fell |
| 2 | Lamps as his dying wish; oil bath | Dying wish is widely told. The oil bath is usually tied to Krishna washing after the battle, so the line gives no reason |
| 3 | Bharat's vow to enter fire, Ram's "so be it" | Valmiki, Ayodhya Kanda 112 |
| 3 | Hanuman arriving as he prepares the fire | Found in secondary accounts. Tulsidas and Valmiki have Hanuman bringing word; I couldn't verify the Kamba version |
| 3 | Return on Kartik Amavasya | Tradition, framed as "कहते हैं". Valmiki's own dating differs, so keep the framing |
| 3 | **Self-harm sensitivity** | Line 02 and 05 involve a vow to enter fire. The hook avoids the word चिता and the picture never shows him near flames. Watch Instagram's handling; if worried, change 02 to "...तो मैं जीवित नहीं रहूँगा" |
| 4 | 7 days × 8 meals = 56 | A popular devotional explanation, not a scriptural rule. Puri's tradition explains 56 differently. Framed with "कहते हैं" |
| 4 | "ब्रज" instead of "गोकुल" | Deliberate: by this episode the family has left Gokul, and commenters notice |
| 5 | Repeated invitations, tilak, meal, boon | Standard katha; versions differ on who the boon protects. **I looked for "souls released from Naraka that day" as a hook and couldn't verify it, so it isn't used** |
| 5 | Yama and Yamuna's mother leaving | Sanjna leaving Surya is a Puranic story, told from memory here, so check it before voicing |
