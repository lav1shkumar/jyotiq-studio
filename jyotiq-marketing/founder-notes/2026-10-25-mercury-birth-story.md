# Founder briefing: Mercury (बुध) nature + birth story
Source: call between Amit Kaushik (founder) and Ayush Rajput, shared 2026-10-25.
Purpose: raw material for 3-4 Jyotiq reels (about a week of content, slotted for the following week).

## 1. Mercury's nature (बुध का स्वभाव)
- Moon hates no one. Mercury hates no one either, but Mercury does not like the Moon (only the Moon).
- Mercury's core problem: no stability (स्थिरता नहीं). "रंग बदलू". Mercury behaves like whoever it sits with.
- Mercury-ruled people (Gemini, Virgo) should choose friends/company very carefully.
- Ayush (Virgo) himself hears this: "तू जिस संगति में बैठता है, वैसा ही बिहेव करने लगता है।"
- Planetary cabinet: Mercury = राजकुमार (prince). Princes are pleasure-loving (अय्याश); good friends make a good prince, bad friends a bad one.
- Mars (सेनापति, commander) dislikes Mercury: the commander's job is defending borders, and he has been handed a prince to babysit.
- How bad this is depends on the chart: if Rahu, Ketu, Shani or Mangal afflict Mercury, the person can lack principles ("सुबह राम-राम, दोपहर में खून"). A strong Mercury = very intelligent, but strong + bad company/afflictions = power goes in a negative direction.
- Mercury = street smartness, लोक व्यवहार, comedy, diplomacy, business, trade.
- Jupiter (बृहस्पति) = wisdom (not mere knowledge), spirituality, deep philosophy, psychology.
- Founder's own example: Amit = Sagittarius (Jupiter), Ayush = Virgo (Mercury).

## 2. Moon background (save for a future Moon deep-dive)
- Moon is Mercury's father, hence Mercury's तेज/lustre.
- When of age, Moon conquered all three worlds and performed a राजसूय यज्ञ (sacrifice of lordship). Details "फिर कभी".
- As a result nine goddesses serve the Moon for life: Lakshmi, Dhriti, Pushti, Srishti, Vapu, Prabha, etc. (beautiful body, nourishment, lustre, excellence, fame, firmness, wealth).
- Founder links these to "नवनिधि" in Hanuman Chalisa ("अष्ट सिद्धि नवनिधि के दाता"); अष्ट सिद्धि = अणिमा, लघिमा, गरिमा etc. (eight powers). Treat this link as the founder's interpretation; verify before stating as fact.
- Moon has 27 wives (the nakshatras); they were hurt by the Tara episode.

## 3. Mercury's birth story (बुध जन्म कथा) – Version 1 (detailed)
- Moon was Jupiter's best disciple.
- Jupiter's wife: Tara (तारा देवी), very beautiful.
- Jupiter, absorbed in his work/wisdom, ignored her. Moon, with his nine treasures, is the most attractive god.
- Tara went with the Moon. Some versions say the Moon took her by force; say "it's not clear, she was with the Moon".
- Jupiter sent repeated messages asking for her return; Moon refused. Jupiter asked Indra; Moon refused Indra too. Indra attacked.
- Shukracharya (Venus, guru of the asuras, always jealous of Jupiter) sided with the Moon; the asuras followed Shukra.
- Sides: Moon + Shukracharya + asuras vs Indra + Jupiter + devas. Rudra (Shiva) sided with Indra/Jupiter because Shiva supports what is right.
- Devas and asuras are brothers: same father, different mothers (Aditi and Diti).
- Brahma (Moon is called Brahma's grandson) scolded the Moon and made him send Tara back.
- Tara returned pregnant. Jupiter: "This can't be mine, you weren't here." Threatened to end the child or her. In the quarrel Tara gave birth.
- The child was extremely radiant, glowing gold. Jupiter loves radiant things (तेज, तपस), so he was delighted and took the child in his lap.
- Moon saw the child and said "it's mine". Paternity dispute.
- Brahma asked Tara privately; she said the Moon is the father.

## 4. Mercury's birth story – Version 2 (short)
- The Tara–Moon relationship is accepted in this version too.
- Once Tara was pregnant, the Moon lost interest: "I can't raise a child, I keep moving, ढाई दिन here, ढाई दिन there. Go home."
- Moon sent Tara back to Jupiter, who scolded her.
- When the beautiful child was born, Jupiter said: "I will raise him like a disciple and give him my name."
- That's why Mercury always respects Jupiter.

## 5. Astrology link the founder wants to use
- Gemini/Virgo lagna people always like Sagittarius/Pisces people, and vice versa: an unspoken relationship of respect (because Jupiter raised Mercury as a disciple).
- Caveat: holds when Mercury, Moon and Jupiter are all well placed; affliction is a separate story.

## 6. Core message for the reels
- Astrologers and Instagram always vilify Shani, Rahu, Ketu, Mangal as पाप ग्रह / malefic (BPHS does call them that).
- But their job is to bring your own पाप out. That's what reels must make people understand.
- The "benefics" (Jupiter, Moon, Mercury, Venus) in this story all acted out of self-interest:
  - Jupiter ignored his wife, fought to get her back, abused her over the child, then claimed the child because it was beautiful (double standard).
  - Moon (the most benefic) had a relationship with another's wife.
  - Venus knew the Moon was wrong but sided with him to settle his grudge against Jupiter.
- Ending line: before judging anyone, put yourself in their shoes and see their role. "कभी किसी दूसरे को जज मत करो।"
- Founder frames this as the astrology DOMAIN's labelling, not the actual planets.
