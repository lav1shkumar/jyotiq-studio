## 5. Separate reel: the festival of light falls on the darkest night
Amit: "ये बहुत इंपॉर्टेंट है। दिस नोट इट डाउन।" Make it a reel of its own.
- Diwali is all about light, yet it falls on अमावस्या, when the Moon has zero light.
- At Diwali the Sun is नीच (debilitated) in तुला (Libra). It is उच्च (exalted) in मेष (Aries) in April, around Akshaya Tritiya.
- The Sun is the source of light: for the world, for health and for the soul. A weak Sun in a chart means weak inside, however strong one looks. The Moon only reflects the Sun's light.
- So on Diwali neither light-giver has strength. The rishis said: light diyas at home, do puja and sadhana, and light the light inside ("अपने अंदर दिवाली लाओ") so outside negativity can't touch you.
- It's the main night for tantra, which is why elders say don't go out on the festival night: the diyas and puja make a protective circle at home. Counter-remedies are done this night too. Amit's framing: "आज बराबर की लड़ाई है": the devas have amrit, but the asuras are strong tonight. ⚠ (tone)
- Holi night is the other such night, but on Holi (Purnima) the Moon is strong.
- Diwali is the one festival celebrated north to south and east to west.

## 9. ⚠ Fact checks before scripting
Things said on the call that viewers would correct in the comments if they go in unchanged:
1. **Ravana's death.** Amit said Ravana was killed on Narak Chaturdashi and Ram reached Ayodhya by Pushpak the next day. The common tradition puts Ravana vadh on Dussehra (Vijayadashami), 20 days before Diwali, and Ram's arrival on Kartik Amavasya, Diwali. Safe line: "रावण वध के बाद राम अयोध्या लौटे".
2. **The dice game on Govardhan day.** The traditional dice game of Kartik Shukla Pratipada (द्यूत प्रतिपदा) is Shiva and Parvati's. The Mahabharata dice game isn't usually tied to this day. Don't state it as fact.
3. **Bhai Dooj story.** The best-known stories are Yamraj visiting his sister Yamuna (hence the name यम द्वितीया) and Krishna visiting Subhadra after killing Narakasura. Draupadi tearing her saree for Krishna is usually told for Raksha Bandhan. Suggest Yama–Yamuna as the main story, since it also echoes Day 2's yam ka deepak. Check with Amit.
4. **The extra month.** The month added roughly every 3 years is अधिक मास (पुरुषोत्तम / मल मास), not चतुर्मास. Chaturmas is the 4 months from Devshayani to Devuthani Ekadashi, linked to Raja Bali and Vaman. Amit's examples fit: 2023 had Adhik Shravan (Diwali 12 Nov), and 2026 had Adhik Jyeshtha (Diwali 8 Nov).
5. **Other calendars.** The Islamic calendar is purely lunar (about 354 days), which is why Ramzan and Eid move about 11 days earlier each year. The Gregorian calendar is purely solar. The Hindu calendar uses both (lunisolar). Compare them neutrally; saying another religion "lacks scientific reasoning" invites hostile comments and reports.
6. **"Darkest night of the year."** Astronomically, every Amavasya is equally moonless. The accurate Jyotish claim: Diwali is the Amavasya when the Sun is also debilitated in Tula, so both light-givers are at their weakest.
7. **Tantra and crime.** Keep the "light your inner diya" message. Drop claims that weak-minded people commit murder on that night or that you'll be caught in tantra if you step out; it's fear-based, and our CTA rule says no fear-selling.
8. **Language count.** Amit said "six languages" but named seven: Hindi, English, Punjabi, Gujarati, Marathi, Spanish, Portuguese. Check the app before putting a number on screen.
9. **Diwali dates.** Confirm all five dates and the Lakshmi Puja muhurat on Drik Panchang before scheduling.
