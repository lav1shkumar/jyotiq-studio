# Jyotiq: Founder Content Guidelines (Amit Kaushik)

A living file. Check it before writing any new Jyotiq reel, script or post.
Source: team call with Amit Kaushik, 26 Oct 2026. Shivansh's remarks on the call were left out on purpose.

## 1. Keep astrology and stories separate
Use a different narrator for each kind of reel.

| Content type | Examples | Narrator (सूत्रधार) |
|---|---|---|
| Astrology / shastra | yogas (e.g. Chandra-Shani yoga), Sade Saati, how to read a placement | बृहत् पाराशर होरा शास्त्र (BPHS) narrator ("मैं बृहत् पाराशर होरा शास्त्र हूँ...") |
| Stories / katha | Raja Harishchandra, Budh's birth, Shani's birth | NOT BPHS. Use the four Kumaras or Narad Muni (below) |

- The founder does not like BPHS as the narrator for a story.
- Using BPHS for everything is fine for now. Move gradually to the separate setup.
- When to start is Ayush's call (today, in 15 days, or later). There is no fixed date.

### Story narrator, option A: the four Kumaras
- They are the four मानस पुत्र of Brahma ji, said to be the most knowledgeable of all. They had so much knowledge that their bodies never needed to grow up, so they stay small children who roam around.
- Names (confirmed by Ayush, 27 Oct 2026): सनक (Sanaka), सनन्दन (Sanandana), सनातन (Sanatana), सनत्कुमार (Sanatkumara).
- Format: the four children talk among themselves. One asks "तुझे ये स्टोरी पता है?", the story plays in the background, another child asks questions, and the story moves forward through their questions.
- Example: "अरे, तुझे पता है, बुध देव, जो बुद्धि के ग्रह हैं, उनका जन्म कैसे हुआ?" / "हाँ मुझे पता है, पर तू बता, तुझे क्या पता है?"
- Tone: they must speak respectfully (इज़्ज़त से). Not casual or slangy.
- Look: illustrated or photo-style children (anime-style also works). The inspiration is a popular Sardar-kid comedy reel format, but ours is knowledge-led, not comedy.

### Story narrator, option B: Narad Muni
- Narad ji alone, entering while playing his वीणा.
- Always opens with "नारायण, नारायण...", then tells the story.
- A single narrator, so all four Kumaras aren't needed.

## 2. Build series planet by planet
- Group reels by planet: Saturn has its set of reels, Mercury has its set, Mars has its set, and so on.
- This way the planet's meaning and its astrology build up naturally across the set.
- Mercury and बुध are the same planet, so they form one series.

## 4. How to ask an LLM for research
- Always write **"As per Vedic astrology"**. Never just "astrology".
- Template (the founder asks this way too):

  > As per Vedic astrology, tell me when Saturn and Moon are together, what kind of yogas are these, what are the positives and negatives of this yoga, and is there any Indian mythological story attached to it?

- The founder will also run the same question and send his answer. Cross-check against it.
