# Jyotiq · बुध Explainer Reel ("हाज़िर जवाब")

Format: 9:16, ~92s (~88s VO + ~4s end-card hold). Built on the edit system of the Chandrama reel (`chandrama_reel_edit_.mp4`), re-skinned for Jyotiq + Mercury emerald.

## 1. What the Chandrama reel does (learned)

**Story spine (90s):** relatable 2 AM scene → "चंचल मन, क्यों?" → every thing has a कारक → मन = चंद्रमा → astronomy proof (nearest, fastest, 2¼ days/rashi, 15+15 phases) → reframe "खराबी नहीं, स्वभाव" → second karaka (जल, 70%) → nature of water (vessel, still/moving) → "मन भी ऐसा ही है" → practical (water settles on its own) → payoff image (still water = whole moon, moving water = broken moon) → quote card "चाँद टूटा नहीं होता। पानी हिल रहा होता है।" → brand end card.

**Frame chrome (on every frame):**
- Header bar: spaced wordmark "ड म रू वा णी" centred, filled orange square left, outline square right. Cream bar on dark scenes, near-black bar on paper scenes.
- Top-right chapter index: a big number that means something (02 = 2 AM, 2¼ days, 15 days, 70%, 03 vessels, 01 whole moon), a Hindi label, and a tiny English translation in tracked caps.
- Footer: bold Hindi subtitle centred (this is the VO line), thin rule left, URL centre, barcode right, two small squares bottom-left.
- Paper grain on both backgrounds.

**Colours (sampled from the video):**
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Night / "inside the mind" background | `#222428` charcoal |
| Explanation background | `#E1D5BD` cream paper |
| Header bar on dark / on paper | `#E6E1CE` / `#14100A` |
| The planet's accent (Moon = cobalt), used for every circle, fill, highlight, strike | `#1F63A8` |
| Brand accent (chrome squares, quote card) | `#EE6A1F` orange |

**Visual language:** engraved/etched objects (lota, kalash, diya, water bowl, drum); halftone B&W portrait for "you"; the deity as a white cut-out sticker on an accent circle; botanical lotus engraving as the opener wipe; black "tape" label boxes, slightly rotated; huge heavy Devanagari titles in two tones (first half cream/black, second half accent: चंचल **मन**, स्व**भाव**).

**Edit types:**
- One layout per idea. The background swaps (charcoal ↔ paper) work as chapter breaks. 13 hard cuts in 90s; everything else animates inside a layout.
- Draw-on lines and arrows (items → कारक circle, deity → lota).
- Fills and counters: one box of 12 fills, the circle fills to 70% while the portrait tints blue at the same time.
- Sequential reveals: moon phases appear left to right, then reverse.
- Strike-through: "खराबी" struck in accent colour, replaced with "नहीं। NOT A FAULT", then "स्वभाव".
- Split screen with a vertical rule to compare two states (left alone | touched, still | moving).
- Diagram motion: orbit diagram with the Moon's orbit highlighted, dial pointer sweeping.
- Captions cross-fade line to line, about 2.5s per line, with a "·" splitting two-part lines.
- Audio: continuous music bed with no gaps (no silence over 0.25s), integrated loudness -14.7 LUFS.

## 4. Sources and checks

**BPHS facts used:**
- बुध = राजकुमार in the planetary cabinet (BPHS ch.3).
- Description: attractive body, "words with many meanings", fond of jokes, a mix of three humours (BPHS 3.26) ([BP Lama Jyotishavidya quoting BPHS 3.26](https://barbarapijan.com/bpa/Graha/Budha/1Budha_main_page.htm)).
- Mercury joined with malefics counts as malefic (BPHS ch.3).
- Elements: Mars fire, Mercury earth, Jupiter ether, Venus water, Saturn air (BPHS ch.3).
- Colour: dūrvā-green.
- Natural relationship: Mercury treats Moon as an enemy; Moon treats Mercury as a friend ([Vic DiCara on Moon & Mercury](https://vicdicara.wordpress.com/2017/08/18/the-saga-of-moon-mercury-understanding-planetary-relationships/)).

**Astronomy:** Mercury's greatest elongation from the Sun is about 18-28°, always under 30°, so in a chart Mercury sits in the Sun's sign or the one next to it.

**Assumptions:**
1. **Voiceover:** I read the reel's frames, not its audio. I treated the bottom subtitle as the voiceover line, because it changes in sync with each beat.
2. **Portrait:** the Chandrama reel uses a real-looking man's photo. For Jyotiq, generate an original face (or use Ayush himself), not a stock or real person.
3. **Brand details:** the header wordmark and footer handle need the Jyotiq site/handle. I don't have the URL.
4. **Quote line:** "बुध ख़राब नहीं होता" is a teaching line, like "चाँद टूटा नहीं होता". In a chart, Mercury can also be weak through debilitation (मीन) or combustion. Amit sir should be OK with the simplification.
5. **Hook:** the hook does not claim that a late comeback means a weak बुध. It only says presence of mind is बुध's domain.
6. **Metal:** left out, because the brass vs bronze question is still open.
