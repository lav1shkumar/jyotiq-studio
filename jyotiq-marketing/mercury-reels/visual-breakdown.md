# Jyotiq Mercury (बुध) reels: visual scene breakdown
Planning only, nothing generated yet. Scripts: the 5 approved Mercury reels (25 Oct 2026). Founder brief: `/Users/ayushrajput/.kiro/crew/workspace/jyotiq-marketing/founder-notes/2026-10-25-mercury-birth-story.md`.
Timings are estimates from the script. Final cuts follow the measured voiceover, as in Ep2-Ep4.

## Shared rules (all 5 reels)

**Look:** same as Ep4. Hand-painted cel anime in the 1993 Indo-Japanese Ramayana style, with soft painted skies, Indian mythological costume and gentle glow. Format is 9:16, 1080x1920, edited at 24fps.

**Mercury grade:** emerald throughout. Base `#0B5D3B`, glow `#50C878`, mint highlight `#A8F0C6`. Shadows go deep green-black, never pure black.

**Character colour keys** (so sound-off viewers can follow who is who):
| Character | Key colour | Design anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Budh (बुध) | emerald `#50C878` | Young prince, green-gold garments, पन्ना on the crown, rides a lion (traditional vahana). As a newborn, shown ONLY as a bundle of light |
| Guru Brihaspati | gold `#D4AF37` | Sage with a white beard, saffron-yellow robes, palm-leaf manuscripts |
| Chandra | silver-white `#E8EEF2` | Serene, crescent on the crown, chariot drawn by antelopes |
| Tara Devi | starlight blue-violet `#8FA8E8` | Dignified, fully draped saree, never sensual |
| Shukracharya | pearl-white `#F4F1EA` + asura indigo `#2E2A6B` | White-robed sage, asura army behind |
| Indra | royal blue + gold | On Airavata (white elephant), vajra |
| Shiva | ash-blue silhouette | Shown only as a silhouette inside light, with trishul and crescent. Never a full character close-up |
| Brahma | lotus pink-gold | Four faces, seated on an open lotus, calm |
| Mangal | red `#C0392B` | Commander in armour |

**Reference stills:** before any Flow shot, make one Nano Banana Pro reference still per character (Budh adult, Guru, Chandra, Tara, Shukra, Mangal). Reuse the same stills across all 5 reels so faces stay consistent, as in Ep4.

**Screen layout** (same positions as Ep1-Ep4 in the Remotion edit):
- Jyotiq logo (`logo-full.png`) top-right, 40px from the right, 168px from the top, 128px wide, for the whole reel.
- Topic title top-centre at about 236px from the top.
- Running captions lower-middle in the series caption style: white, with the key word highlighted in emerald.
- Hook text for the first ~3s is big, in the middle third.
- Bottom 250px and the right rail stay clear.
- The Flow ✦ watermark stays visible. Never cover it.

**Text:** every word on screen is added in the edit. None is generated inside Flow or Nano Banana (the models garble text, Devanagari especially).

**Opening rule:** the first frame is always one lit emerald element. Never open on black.

**Ending:** the series ending from Ep1-Ep4. The last shot ends on a light shape that becomes a phone, and the Jyotiq app is revealed when the narrator says "Jyotiq", then the end card holds ~1.5s. For Mercury, the sparks and glow are recoloured emerald. The logo keeps its own colours.

**Guardrails:**
- Tara and Chandra never touch. Their story is told through light and walking.
- No birth scene. The newborn is a cloth-wrapped bundle of light with no visible baby, because Flow's minors policy rejected two child shots in Ep4.
- Deities stay dignified. Meme framing (case numbers, red flags, POV) lives only in the text overlays.
- Celebrities appear as faceless silhouettes only, with no team kit, logo or jersey number.
- Sound: only relevant diegetic sound under the VO, as in Ep4.
