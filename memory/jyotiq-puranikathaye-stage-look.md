On 2026-11-19 Ayush sent `~/Downloads/Kilavde7.mp4`, a screen recording of the @puranikathaye Instagram page showing its Bhairav, Tara, Bhairavi and Samudra Manthan reels. He wants to "100% copy" its visuals for a reel about Kumbhakarna wrenching out Airavat's tusk and striking Indra's chest.

The look, decoded:
- **Photographic devotional stage theatre:** a hand-painted canvas backdrop, a plaster stage floor with marigold petals, a havan fire, haze, and one cool-blue follow-spot beam.
- **Greasepaint and latex characters:** glowing irises and slow blood drips.
- **Shot grammar:** ECU face, then a macro prop insert, then a wide tableau.
- **Impact:** a spark burst.
- **Sound:** a dark near-mono drone and heartbeat bed, and a silence gap before each sub-drop hit.

The analysis and frame/Veo prompts are in a session scratch folder: `kumbhakarna-airavat/PROMPTS.md` plus `refs/`. It may not have been kept.

**Why:** this look conflicts with the founder's 10 Nov "scripture designs, not TV look" rule ([[jyotiq-scriptural-character-designs]]). The pack copies the camera, lighting, set and sound but keeps text-based character designs.

**How to apply:**
- In Valmiki, the tusk strike is Kumbhakarna's own clash with Indra (Yuddha Kanda, around sarga 61), not Ravana's. Ravana carries Airavat tusk scars (Aranya 32). Confirm both in Gita Press.
- Give prompts only; Ayush makes the frames in GPT. Ask before any Veo spend ([[veo-vertex-route]]).

**Update, 19 Nov:** Ayush shared `~/Downloads/Puranikathaye_Claude_Director_Playbook.md`, an 89-reel audit with Modes A/B/C, prompt contracts, music cues and QA. Ep1 "Why did Kumbhakarna sleep six months?" is in `kumbhakarna-airavat/EPISODE-01.md`, built by `build_episode.py` (the locks live in the script).
