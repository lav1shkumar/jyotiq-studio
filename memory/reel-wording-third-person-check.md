Before generating voice or rendering any Jyotiq reel, read every spoken line and every on-screen chip/caption as a third-person viewer and fix phrasing that is unnatural or slightly off, even in a script he approved.

**Why:** On 20 Nov 2026 Ayush caught "पर अंबा का मन राजा शाल्व को दे चुका था।" in the rendered भारत Ep3 and corrected it to "पर अंबा का मन राजा शाल्व पर था।" He asked me to keep myself as a third person to catch such wording mistakes.

**How to apply:** Make it a step before the voice step in [[jyotiq-myth-script-skill]] and [[bharata-ep1-build]]. Prefer plain spoken idiom ("का मन X पर था") over literary or translated constructions. If a line in an approved script reads wrong, flag it with a suggested fix before spending credits, instead of silently voicing it.
