Panel 08 (the bow at the guru's feet): text says Bhishma "came down from my car and placing my bow aside ... proceeded on foot" (182). Drawn as a standing pranam with joined hands.

## Script accuracy notes from the read (no wording changed yet)
- Verified: line 4 ("In those days no Bhishma was born", 181), line 8 ("I cannot, however, wish thee victory, for I myself stand here to vanquish thee", 182), line 12 (Prasvapa "known to thee in thy former life", 186), line 14 (Narada, 188), line 16 (Rama to Amba, 189), line 17 (Amba's resolve, 189).
- Line 1: the text's threat is "I will slay thee this very day along with all thy counsellors" (181), close to "वरना आज तुम्हारा वध होगा".
- Line 13 "तेईसवें दिन": the text gives 23 days in all (185), then the dream night, then Prasvapa "next" (187). Either keep "तेईसवें दिन" as the counter's last day or say "अगली सुबह". Ayush to choose.
- Amba's own wish at the ashram was for Bhishma to be slain (179–180); line 6 "न्याय कीजिए" is a softer paraphrase.
