## Retention plan (why viewers stay to the end)
- Frame 0 is the clash, not narration. The "भीष्म पैदा नहीं हुआ था" comeback lands by ~10 s.
- The open question "जीता कौन?" is on the hook plate and is answered only at line 19.
- A day counter (दिन 1 → 23) stays on screen through the fight, so the viewer always knows the end is coming.
- A turn every 8–12 s: the guru refuses to bless, Bhishma falls, the dream astra, Narada stops him, the guru admits it.
- The last line hands over a new question (who can bring Bhishma down?), answered by Ep3 viewers in the comments: शिखंडी.

## Source note
- Incident and source: Mahabharata, Udyoga Parva, Ambopakhyana Parva (Ganguli ch. 178–186), told by Bhishma himself. Scriptural.
- In the text: Amba goes to the ascetics; Akritavrana and Hotravahana bring her to Parashurama, Bhishma's weapons guru. Parashurama orders Bhishma to take her back and threatens to kill him if he refuses. Bhishma refuses, saying he won't abandon his duty out of fear. They fight at Kurukshetra; Bhishma bows and asks for blessing first; Ganga tries to stop her son (cut from v1 for pace; optional add-back above). The fight runs 23 days; Bhishma is struck and falls senseless. Eight brahmanas appear to him in a dream and give him the Prasvapa weapon, known to him from a former life. Narada and the gods stop him from using it against his guru; Parashurama's ancestors tell him to lay down his weapons. Parashurama tells Amba he could not defeat Bhishma and advises her to seek Bhishma's protection; she refuses and goes off to do tapas to kill him herself.
- Dramatisation: every spoken line is a paraphrase, not a quote. "तब भीष्म पैदा नहीं हुआ था" closely follows Bhishma's own boast in the text (the kshatriyas Parashurama defeated lived when there was no Bhishma), but check the exact wording before it goes on the hook plate. "विजयी भव नहीं कहूँगा" paraphrases Parashurama's reply when asked for a blessing. The eight brahmanas are shown with a callback to the Vasus; the text doesn't name them as Vasus, so the picture hints and the voiceover doesn't claim it.
- Simplified: in the text Ganga also takes the reins of Bhishma's chariot after he falls, and there are several faints on both sides. Kept to one fall for time.
- Uncertainty: chapter numbers vary between editions (Ganguli 178–186 in most); verify the Ganga and dream details against the BORI critical edition before production.
- Third-person wording check: done (see below).

## Wording check (outside viewer, 25 Nov)
- "वरना आज तुम्हारा वध निश्चित है" is formal, but it suits Parashurama's register; kept.
- "वो गिर पड़े", "भीष्म रुक गए" keep the honorific plural for Bhishma, as in Ep2/Ep3.
- "जो गुरु को रणभूमि में सुला देता": a viewer may read "सुला देना" as "kill". That ambiguity helps the tension, and the text's weapon does put the enemy to sleep (a humiliation, not death), so it stays; on screen the chip says "प्रस्वाप · नींद का अस्त्र".
- "जब मन दो राहों पर अटके" is plain spoken idiom; no promise of an answer, only "पूछिए".
